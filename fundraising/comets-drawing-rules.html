Prize Drawing: Official Rules

1. Sponsor

The Colorado Comets Youth Baseball Organization, a Colorado nonprofit corporation recognized as tax-exempt under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (the "Sponsor").

2. Who can enter

The drawing is open to people who are 18 or older and live in Colorado. Coaches, board members and the members of their households are not eligible to win. One person may win only one prize.

3. Entry period

From November 1, 2026 at 12:00 a.m. Mountain Time to November 30, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Mountain Time.

4. How to enter

Free entry. You may enter without donating, by any one of these methods. A free entry earns 1 entry, the same as an entry made with a donation.

  • Online: complete the entry form at cometbaseball.com/fundraising/comets-drawing-entry.html with your name, phone number and email.
  • In person: ask any Comets volunteer.

Limit one entry per person per day, whether you enter free or with a donation.

Donation entry. A donation of any amount earns 1 entry. Online and in person donations are entered automatically using the name and contact information given with the donation. A larger donation does not earn more entries.

Sweepstakes entries are limited to one per person per day. You may donate online at https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/donation-form/colorado-comets-sweepstakes, by cash or check made out to Colorado Comets, or in person to a Comets volunteer. Donations are voluntary gifts to the Sponsor and are not refundable. The Sponsor is a 501(c)(3) organization. Please ask your tax advisor how to treat your donation. A receipt is available on request.

All entries, free and donated, go into the same drawing and are treated the same.

5. Prizes

Prizes are donated by local businesses. Values are approximate retail values. Additional prizes may be added during the entry period. Any added prize will be listed in the table below and posted on cometbaseball.com by November 17, 2026, and will be drawn from the same pool of entries. All prizes will be listed before the entry period ends.

If a listed prize becomes unavailable, the Sponsor may substitute a prize of equal or greater value. The Sponsor will not remove a listed prize or reduce the number of prizes.

Prizes have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for cash, and cannot be transferred. The donating business sets the terms of each prize. The Sponsor does not provide or guarantee any prize. Winners are responsible for any taxes on a prize.

6. The drawing

The drawing will take place on or before December 20, 2026. At least two adult Comets volunteers will draw one entry at random from the single pool of all entries for each prize, in the order listed in Section 5, with any added prizes drawn last. If a name is drawn for a second prize, it is set aside and another entry is drawn. You do not need to be present to win.

Your chances of winning depend on how many entries the Sponsor receives.

7. Winners

Winners will be contacted by phone and email within 3 days of the drawing. A winner who does not reply within 7 days forfeits the prize, and another entry will be drawn. Prizes are claimed from the Sponsor or the donating business as directed. By accepting a prize, the winner agrees that the Sponsor may share the winner's first name and town. A winners list is available by emailing info@cometbaseball.com after February 1, 2027.

8. Your information

We collect your name, phone number and email only to run the drawing, notify winners and give donation receipts. We do not sell or share your information.

9. General conditions

By entering, you agree to these rules and to the Sponsor's decisions, which are final. The Sponsor may disqualify anyone who tampers with the entry process or breaks these rules. The Sponsor is not responsible for lost, late, incomplete or misdirected entries, or for technical failures. If the drawing cannot be run as planned, the Sponsor may change or cancel it and will post a notice at cometbaseball.com. Winners release the Sponsor and the donating businesses from claims arising from the prize.

10. Questions

Email info@cometbaseball.com.
