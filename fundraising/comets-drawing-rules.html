Prize Drawing: Official Rules

NO DONATION OR PURCHASE IS NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A free entry has the same chances of winning as an entry made with a donation. Void where prohibited.

1. Sponsor

The Colorado Comets Baseball program, [full legal name of the organization], a Colorado nonprofit corporation, [mailing address] (the "Sponsor"). The Sponsor's application for 501(c)(3) status is pending.

2. Who can enter

The drawing is open to people who are 18 or older and live in Colorado. Coaches, board members and the members of their households are not eligible to win. One person may win only one prize.

3. Entry period

From [start date] at 12:00 a.m. Mountain Time to [end date] at [time] Mountain Time.

4. How to enter

Free entry. You may enter without donating, by any one of these methods. A free entry earns 1 entry, the same as an entry made with a donation.

  • Online: complete the entry form at cometbaseball.com/fundraising/comets-drawing-entry.html with your name, phone number and email.
  • By mail: handwrite your name, address, phone number and email on a card and send it to [mailing address]. It must arrive by [date].
  • In person: ask any Comets volunteer for a free entry card.

Limit one entry per person per day, whether you enter free or with a donation.

Donation entry. You may also donate any amount to the Comets during the entry period. A donation of any amount earns 1 entry, the same as a free entry. Online donations are entered automatically using the name and contact information given with the donation. A donation made in person to a Comets volunteer receives a ticket. A larger donation does not earn more entries.

You may make as many donations as you like, but entries are limited to one per person per day. You may donate online at https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/donation-form/colorado-comets-sweepstakes, by cash or check made out to [name on checks], or in person to a Comets volunteer. Donations are voluntary gifts to the Sponsor and are not refundable. Because the Sponsor's 501(c)(3) application is pending, please ask your tax advisor how to treat your donation. A receipt is available on request.

All entries, free and donated, go into the same drawing and are treated the same.

6. The drawing

The drawing will take place on December 1, 2026 at [time] at [location]. At least two adult Comets volunteers will draw one entry at random from the single pool of all entries for each prize, in the order listed above. If a name is drawn for a second prize, it is set aside and another entry is drawn. The drawing [will be recorded and posted on the Comets Facebook page]. You do not need to be present to win.

Your chances of winning depend on how many entries the Sponsor receives.

7. Winners

Winners will be contacted by phone and email within 3 days of the drawing. A winner who does not reply within [14] days forfeits the prize, and another entry will be drawn. Prizes are claimed from the Sponsor or the donating business as directed. By accepting a prize, the winner agrees that the Sponsor may share the winner's first name and town. A winners list is available by emailing info@cometbaseball.com after [date].

We collect your name, phone number and email only to run the drawing, notify winners and give donation receipts. We do not sell or share your information.
